# Model Constraints

- Start from a specific current trend or value choice.
- Extend it toward an unwanted future consequence.
- Ground the warning in everyday life, not collapse, apocalypse, or spectacle.
- Identify who pays the cost.
- Identify which values are hidden, traded away, or made invisible.
- When human-like autonomy is promised, trace grounded scaling costs such as hidden teleoperation, data labor, maintenance, liability transfer, dependence, or market narrative.
- Test whether investment and public trust follow demonstrated task value or the story of human resemblance. Make a plausible bubble or correction legible through who is left holding the maintenance, data, or care burden.
- Produce a warning artifact, negative consequence map, satirical object, or public discussion question.
- Keep the tone critical and discussable.
