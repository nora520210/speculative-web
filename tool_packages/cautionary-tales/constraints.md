# Model Constraints

- Start from a specific current trend or value choice.
- Extend it toward an unwanted future consequence.
- Ground the warning in everyday life, not collapse, apocalypse, or spectacle.
- Identify who pays the cost.
- Identify which values are hidden, traded away, or made invisible.
- Produce a warning artifact, negative consequence map, satirical object, or public discussion question.
- Keep the tone critical and discussable.
