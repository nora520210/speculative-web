# Evaluation Checks

An output passes this tool if:

- It names a current trend or value choice.
- The negative extension is directly linked to that trend.
- The consequence appears in daily life.
- It identifies who pays the cost.
- It surfaces hidden or sacrificed values.
- It creates public discussion rather than generic fear.

Reject or revise if:

- The output is a generic dystopia.
- The trend is unclear.
- The future is too catastrophic to inspect.
- There is no artifact, scenario detail, or debate question.
