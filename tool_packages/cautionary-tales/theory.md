# Cautionary Tales

## Source Logic

This tool intentionally presents an unwanted future to warn people about the possible consequences of a trend or value choice. It should not become a generic disaster story. The warning must be grounded in everyday life.

The tool is useful when an issue is wrapped in excessive optimism, technological solutionism, or commercial inevitability. It uses negative futures, satire, and dark design to create critical distance.

## When To Use

- A trend is being promoted as obviously beneficial.
- A project needs to expose who pays the cost of a future.
- The discussion needs warning, critique, or discomfort without collapsing into spectacle.

## Key Question

What is the most dangerous extension of this trend, who pays the price, and which values are being hidden?

## Misuse Risks

- Producing generic dystopia.
- Making the future too catastrophic to discuss.
- Losing the link to a specific trend or value choice.
- Warning without giving the audience a question to debate.
