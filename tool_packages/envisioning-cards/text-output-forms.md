# Text Output Forms

Source basis: `思辨设计tools-3.pdf`, Envisioning Cards / 远景卡片, pages 18-19.

## Default Form

Use four clusters of prompt cards and one workshop check-out question.

1. **Stakeholder cards.** Ask who is absent, burdened, made responsible, or unable to refuse.
2. **Time cards.** Move between immediate, delayed, long-term, and post-adoption effects.
3. **Value cards.** Surface conflicts such as autonomy versus safety or legibility versus privacy without resolving them.
4. **Diffusion cards.** Ask what happens if the proposal becomes ordinary, mandatory, copied, or infrastructural.
5. **Workshop check-out.** End with one question about the evidence or design change needed before proceeding.

## Selection Rule

Prefer this form after a concept, scenario, prototype, or artifact exists and needs group critique.

## Rendering Contract

Emit four titled `questions` blocks followed by one `callout`. Keep prompts concise, concrete, and answerable in a workshop; never return a completed ethics verdict.
