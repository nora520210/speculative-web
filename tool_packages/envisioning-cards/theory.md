# Envisioning Cards

## Source Logic

Envisioning Cards are prompts that help researchers examine stakeholders, time scales, values, and longer-term effects of technologies or design proposals.

In this system, the tool should act as an ethics and reflection layer around generated scenarios, artifacts, or scientific concepts.

## When To Use

- A concept needs critique before image or artifact generation.
- The user wants workshop prompts or review questions.
- A scenario may ignore affected groups, values, or long-term diffusion.

## Recommended Inputs

Use a concept, proposal, prototype description, generated scenario, or image semantic summary.

## Recommended Outputs

Text is recommended: card prompts, question sets, and review checklists. Image output can later render the cards as an artifact or workshop scene.

## Misuse Risks

- Turning cards into generic ethical advice.
- Missing concrete stakeholders.
- Asking questions that do not change design decisions.
