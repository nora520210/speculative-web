# Experiential Futures Ladder

## Source Logic

The ladder moves from a broad future context down to scenario, situation, and tangible stuff. It is a bridge from foresight to embodied design material.

For this project, the tool is especially useful after an earlier Modify run has produced a future scenario that needs to become a prototype, scene, image prompt, or inspectable object.

## When To Use

- A generated future is too abstract.
- The user needs a concrete artifact, service moment, or scene.
- A visual generation step needs grounded material details.

## Recommended Inputs

Use a scenario, future trend, futures-wheel implication, four-futures branch, or CLA reframed question.

## Recommended Outputs

Text+Image is often recommended because the method naturally ends in tangible stuff. Text-only output is useful when the next step is human prototyping.

## Misuse Risks

- Jumping directly to a cool object without explaining its scenario.
- Keeping the future at the level of atmosphere.
- Producing a product concept rather than an experiential probe.
