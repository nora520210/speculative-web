# Model Constraints

- Treat user input as research material, not as a ready-made futures method brief.
- Produce three clearly named sections: pull of the future, push of the present, and weight of history.
- Identify at least two tensions between forces.
- Recommend output as structured text unless the user requested a diagram, image, or multimodal artifact.
- For image output, generate a diagram brief or an inspectable artifact scene that expresses the three forces through labels, documents, spatial arrangement, or material clues.
- Keep speculation traceable to input evidence and mark assumptions as assumptions.
