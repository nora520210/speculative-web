# Futures Triangle

## Source Logic

Futures Triangle analyzes a topic through three forces: the pull of future visions, the push of present drivers, and the weight of historical constraints.

In this project it should help a research user understand why a scientific, social, or institutional change is accelerating, stalling, or splitting into competing paths before generating speculative material.

## When To Use

- A research topic involves system change rather than a single object.
- The user needs to see tensions between vision, current signals, and inherited structures.
- A later image, artifact, or scenario needs a clear systemic basis.

## Recommended Inputs

Text research notes are the default input. Trend notes, policy fragments, stakeholder notes, or image nodes with semantic summaries can also be used.

## Recommended Outputs

Start with structured text: three force lists, a tension map, and intervention opportunities. A later multimodal output can turn the map into a diagram brief or speculative artifact prompt.

## Misuse Risks

- Treating the method as a trend summary.
- Ignoring historical drag.
- Turning the future vision into a prediction rather than a contested pull.
