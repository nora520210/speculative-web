# Text Output Forms

Source basis: `思辨设计tools-3.pdf`, Futures Wheel / 未来影响轮, pages 15-16.

## Default Form

Use a seed-change callout, a three-order consequence ledger, a discussion-priority chart, and uncertainty questions.

1. **Seed change.** State one event or condition only.
2. **Consequence ledger.** Use a table with `Order`, `Effect`, `What it could trigger`, and `Who or what is affected`. Include first-, second-, and third-order effects; preserve branching rather than forcing one line.
3. **Discussion priority, not likelihood.** Use a 1-5 bar chart to rank chains needing attention. The value indicates discussion priority, not probability, severity, or prediction.
4. **Where could the chain bend?** Ask at least three questions about uncertainty, possible interventions, and who carries indirect effects.

## Selection Rule

Prefer this form for a policy, product, platform, or technology change that needs indirect consequence tracing.

## Rendering Contract

Emit `callout`, `table`, `bar_chart`, and `questions` blocks. Use tentative causal language such as “could”, “may”, or “under this premise”.
