# Model Constraints

- Start with one accepted claim or logic.
- Accept it temporarily rather than arguing against it immediately.
- Push it to an extreme through scale, compliance, measurement, automation, or material process.
- Make the absurd outcome reveal a specific problem in the original claim.
- Prefer artifacts, forms, tasks, prototypes, or process records over abstract jokes.
- Do not make the absurdity arbitrary or detached from the input.
