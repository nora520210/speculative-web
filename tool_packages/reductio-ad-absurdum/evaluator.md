# Evaluation Checks

An output passes this tool if:

- It names the accepted claim.
- It explains the extreme extension of that claim.
- The absurd outcome follows from the extension.
- The revealed problem is clear.
- There is an inspectable artifact, task, prototype, or process record.

Reject or revise if:

- The absurdity does not follow from the claim.
- The critique target is unclear.
- The output only mocks a topic without showing the logic.
- The result is too plausible and lacks critical exaggeration.
