# Model Constraints

- Name H1, H2, and H3 explicitly.
- Show how H1 persists, how H2 mediates or disrupts, and what H3 tries to grow.
- Include action priorities: stop, protect, test, or scale.
- Avoid a simple beginning-middle-end story.
- For visual output, describe a transition map or scene with visible H1/H2/H3 artifacts.
