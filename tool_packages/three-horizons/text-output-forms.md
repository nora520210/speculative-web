# Text Output Forms

Source basis: `思辨设计tools-3.pdf`, Three Horizons / 三地平线, pages 19-20.

## Default Form

Use a three-horizons transition table, a stop/protect/try action portfolio, and transition questions.

1. **Transition across three horizons.** Compare H1, H2, and H3 using `What dominates or emerges`, `Signal or practice`, and `Value at risk`.
2. **Action portfolio.** Use a table with `Stop, protect, or try`, `Action`, `Why now`, and `First low-risk test`.
3. **Transition tensions to keep open.** Ask at least three questions that retain conflict between present obligations and emerging possibilities.

## Selection Rule

Prefer this form when a desired future needs to be connected back to present transition choices.

## Rendering Contract

Emit two `table` blocks and one `questions` block. H1, H2, and H3 are coexisting forces, not a simple linear timeline.
