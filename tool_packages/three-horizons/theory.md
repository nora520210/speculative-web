# Three Horizons

## Source Logic

Three Horizons maps a dominant current system, transitional innovations, and a long-term emerging future in relation to one another.

In this system it helps convert speculative visions back into present actions: what to stop, what to protect, and what to test.

## When To Use

- A future scenario needs a transition path.
- The user wants to connect long-term vision to present experiments.
- The project needs to compare current systems and emerging practices.

## Recommended Inputs

Use a system description, transition goal, scenario, policy context, or generated speculative output.

## Recommended Outputs

Text is recommended first: H1, H2, H3, tensions, and action priorities. A diagram specification can be generated as text or as a later image prompt.

## Misuse Risks

- Treating the horizons as a simple chronological timeline.
- Ignoring current system persistence.
- Producing vision without near-term action.
