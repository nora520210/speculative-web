# What-If Scenarios

## Source Logic

This tool turns a broad research topic, trend, or ethical issue into a sharp speculative condition. It uses a "What if..." question to compress complexity into a discussable future premise.

The tool should help the user move from an abstract issue to everyday consequences: who benefits, who is harmed, what new services or rules appear, and what unfairness or value conflict becomes visible.

## When To Use

- The issue is too broad or abstract.
- The team needs to concretize ethical and social implications.
- A topic needs to become a short scenario, script, object list, or discussion prompt.

## Key Question

If one condition really changed, how would ordinary bodies, relationships, labor, consumption, or governance be reorganized?

## Misuse Risks

- Producing a vague trend summary instead of a specific changed condition.
- Treating the scenario as a prediction rather than a speculative probe.
- Making the future too generic to generate objects or debate.
